Vodka Pasta

Vodka Pasta is a creamy Italian-American dish made with a tomato-based vodka sauce and pasta.

Ingredients

  • Pasta (penne or rigatoni)
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Heavy Cream
  • Vodka
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Red Pepper Flakes (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta according to package instructions and set aside.
  2. Sauté minced garlic in olive oil over medium heat until fragrant.
  3. Add tomato sauce and vodka to the pan, stirring to combine. Simmer for 5 minutes to cook off the alcohol.
  4. Stir in heavy cream and red pepper flakes (if desired) for a creamy consistency.
  5. Toss the cooked pasta in the sauce and top with parmesan cheese.

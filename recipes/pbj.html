Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

A Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich is a simple and classic American snack made with peanut butter and jelly spread between slices of bread.

Ingredients

  • Bread (any type)
  • Peanut Butter
  • Jelly (your favorite flavor)
  • Butter (optional)

Steps

  1. Spread peanut butter evenly on one slice of bread.
  2. Spread jelly evenly on the other slice of bread.
  3. Press the two slices together, with the spreads facing inward.
  4. Optional: Toast the sandwich in a pan with butter for a warm, crispy treat.

Chicken Parmesan

Chicken Parmesan is an italian dish that consists of a fried chicken cutlet topped with marinara and parmesan cheese

Ingredients

  • Chicken Breast (butterflied)
  • Marinara Sauce
  • Parmesan
  • Oil
  • Eggs
  • Bread Crumbs
  • Salt & Pepper

Steps

  1. Dip the breast chicken breast into egg wash to ensure it is covered.
  2. Dip the breast into bread crumbs till everything is covered
  3. Pan fry the breast on both sides till golden brown and cooked internally
  4. Apply marinara sauce and parmesan to cheese on top and broil for 2 mins on high.
